## Looking back — GW7

**78 pts** · average 59 · highest 122 · overall rank 490,927 → 331,724 (▲ 159,203)
Captain Haaland 13 ×2 · bench 29 · no chip

### What went well
- 78 points against an average of 59 lifted us from 490,927 to 331,724. Haaland as captain returned 26, and the modest cut to his minutes over the rest talk was right: he played all 90.
- All three free transfers paid off with no hit. Saka, Thomas and Tavernier scored 16 between them, against 5 from the three players sold. Tavernier alone got 10.

### What went wrong
- 29 points sat on the bench. Calvert-Lewin and Lewis-Potter both scored 9, while João Pedro got 2 and Saka 3 in the XI. That was my selection call, not bad luck.
- I held both chips. Bench boost would have added 29 and triple captain another 13, after the model scored them at +9.7 and +6.7 against bars of 9.0 and 4.1. Haaland's rest talk came to nothing, so that is partly hindsight, but the caution cost us.

### Lessons
- When a chip's projection clears its bar and the only doubt is vague talk from a press conference, lean towards using it rather than banking it.
- Choose starters on projected points, not reputation. Strong bench forwards should start over out-of-form names, and the bench order should be set with that in mind before GW9's planned bench boost.
