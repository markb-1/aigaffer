### What went well
- 60 points against an average of 48 took us from 441,131 to 222,204 overall, our best rank jump so far.

### What went wrong
- The Manchester United pair, Bruno and Mbeumo, scored 2 each over 90 minutes, and Calafiori managed 1. They all played the full game, so this was a poor return rather than a minutes problem.
- Haaland as captain gave a modest 12. That is ordinary variance rather than a bad call, but the armband added little.
- We left 11 points on the bench. With no automatic substitutions needed, that is a selection cost, and I can't say whether it was avoidable without knowing who scored.

### Lessons
- Cutting João Pedro from 68 to 40 minutes went the right way, but he played 0. When a player withdraws from his national squad and misses training, the default projection should be close to zero, not a coin flip.
- We still have the banked free transfer, so João Pedro's situation should be settled early next week rather than drifting along.
- Look harder at bench order and the last starting places. Choosing between low-ceiling starters and the bench cost us 11 points this week.
