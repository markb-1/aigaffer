## Looking back — GW11

**35 pts** · average 35 · highest 106 · overall rank 603,048 → 649,208 (▼ 46,160)
Captain Haaland 2 ×2 (best in the XI: Calvert-Lewin 9) · bench 30 · no chip

### What went well
- Calvert-Lewin (9) and Tavernier (8) carried the XI, and both had been confirmed fit before the deadline. That gave us 35 points, level with the average.
- Selling the injured João Pedro for N.Jackson gained 2 points with no hit. That was a small gain, but the right move.

### What went wrong
- We left 30 points on the bench, including Lewis-Potter's 16 and Tzolakis's 10. Meanwhile Vuskovic, Konsa, Gabriel and Mbeumo scored 3 between them. Those were our selection calls, and they cost far more than any bad luck did.
- Captain Haaland blanked for 4 points at home to Fulham. That was bad luck on a sound pick, but together with the bench it dropped us from 603k to 649k.

### Lessons
- The starting XI and bench order should follow form and projections, not reputation. When a bench player like Lewis-Potter is in form, he needs a hard look before a low-ceiling defender like Vuskovic or Konsa starts ahead of him.
- Our own minutes doubts should shape the lineup. We cut Konsa to 70 minutes and still started him, so a rotation-risk defender should not start over a fit alternative.
