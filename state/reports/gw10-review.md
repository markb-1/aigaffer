## Looking back — GW10

**66 pts** · average 55 · highest 139 · overall rank 654,720 → 603,048 (▲ 51,672)
Captain Haaland 2 ×2 (best in the XI: Vuskovic 12) · bench 9 · no chip

### What went well
- The defence carried the week. Vuskovic 12, Muharemović 10, Gabriel 8 and Konsa 8 helped us to 66 against an average of 55, and our rank rose from 654,720 to 603,048.
- Konsa coming in for Thomas gained a net 8 points. Even though I'd cut his minutes to 60 over doubts about his place, he played the full 90.

### What went wrong
- Captaining Haaland returned only 4 points while Vuskovic, Groß and Muharemović each scored 10 or more. He played 90 minutes, so this was bad luck rather than a mistake in the pick.
- Guéhi went off at half-time with 0 points, after I'd raised his minutes to 75 based only on a predicted XI. Mbeumo, Tavernier and Rice managed just 8 between them.

### Lessons
- Don't raise a player's minutes on a predicted XI alone. Guéhi showed how little that evidence is worth, so ask for press-conference or club confirmation first.
- Starting Verbruggen over Tzolakis made no difference, since both scored 6. That suggests our goalkeepers are close to interchangeable, so the free transfer we rolled is better spent elsewhere.
