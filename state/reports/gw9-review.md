## Looking back — GW9

**39 pts** · average 47 · highest 122 · overall rank 263,810 → 654,720 (▼ 390,910)
Captain Haaland 2 ×2 (best in the XI: Rice 8) · bench 11 · no chip

### What went well
- Rice was our best starter with 8, and Vuskovic came on automatically for Guéhi and scored 6, so the blank in defence cost us nothing.
- The transfer cost no hit, and Gabriel got the same 6 points as Calafiori, so the move broke even.

### What went wrong
- I started Tzolakis (1) over Verbruggen (11). That goalkeeper choice cost us 10 points and is the biggest reason we finished 8 below the average of 47.
- Haaland played 90 minutes but scored only 2 as captain. That was bad luck, not bad minutes. My minutes calls were wrong twice: Calafiori played 90 when I expected 15, and Guéhi played 0 when I expected 65.

### Lessons
- Before using a transfer on a flagged player, check for newer news than an old report. My Calafiori downgrade was based on news from days earlier, and he started.
- Compare both goalkeepers each week and start the better one. Here the one I benched was the Brighton keeper I had noted was fresh.
